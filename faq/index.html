FAQ

Interested in the Bee for 2026-27? Here's some information you might find useful! If you have any further questions, please reach out to us at team@nationalgeobee.org.

We’re an organization dedicated to promoting geographical knowledge and education nationwide. Our primary focus is organizing the National Geography Bee, a nationwide competition for high schoolers that aims to inspire and build curiosity about the people and places around them—including their home state, the United States as a whole, and the world at large. We are officially registered as a 501(c)3 nonprofit organization in the state of Massachusetts.

We are completely independent of and totally unaffiliated with National Geographic, which used to run a geography bee for fourth through eighth graders until 2020, as well as any other organization that runs geography competitions at any level.

Registration for the 2027 National Geography Bee will close on December 18, 2026. The school-level competition will kick off on January 3, 2027 and run until February 26. School champions will take the National Qualifying Test between March 1 and March 5, and the top scorers will compete in the National Finals in late April 2027.

The National Geography Bee will be offering early bird discounts for schools that sign up in the earlier weeks of our registration period. We are also pleased to offer a $20 discount to all schools that participated in 2026! The entry fees for the 2027 National Geography Bee are as follows:

  • Until October 1, 2026: $95
  • From October 1 until November 18: $125
  • From November 18 through December 18: $150
